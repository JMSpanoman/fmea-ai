## Purpose

To define **[Company Legal Name]** (“the Organization”) requirements for **creating, maintaining, reviewing, and archiving** the **Design History File (DHF)** so the organization can **objectively demonstrate** that design and development was **performed in accordance with** **approved** procedures and **plans,** in accordance with **ISO 13485:2016, clause 4.2.4** and, through the **7.3** design and development **records** the DHF **includes** or **references,** the applicable requirements of **section 7.3** for the **device, software, or type** in scope.

## Scope

### In scope

- The **Design History File** for **[each device, device type, or family — define,** e.g. per project code, UDI-DI, or technical file line]**: the **records** (or a **controlled index and pointers** to records) that demonstrate that design and development was planned and performed in accordance with the requirements of **4.2.4,** in **paper,** **electronic,** or **hybrid** form.
