## Purpose

To define **[Company Legal Name]** (“the Organization”) requirements for planning, implementing, verifying, validating, and maintaining **risk management** for medical devices and related services, in accordance with **ISO 14971:2019** and the Organization’s **QMS** under **ISO 13485:2016**.

This SOP ensures risks are **identified, analyzed, evaluated, controlled**, and **reviewed** over the **product lifecycle**, including **post-production** activity, and that evidence is maintained in a **risk management file (RMF)** suitable for **notified body** and **regulatory** review.

The process elements in this SOP, aligned with **ISO 14971:2019** and the QMS per **ISO 13485:2016** where they apply, are: **risk management planning,** **hazard identification,** **risk analysis,** **risk evaluation,** **risk control,** **residual risk assessment,** **benefit–risk analysis** when required, **risk management report,** **risk management review,** and **review of production and post-production information.**

## Procedure

### General requirements

1. Risk management shall be **sustained** through the lifecycle and linked to **[SOP-### Design and development]** and **[SOP-### Design and development changes]**.
2. For each **[device / product family]**, the Organization shall maintain:
   - A **risk management plan (RMP)** per ISO 14971 4.4: scope, responsibilities, **review** requirements, **risk acceptability** criteria, **verification/validation** approaches for controls, and **post-production** information review.
   - A **RMF** with (or references to) evidence for: analysis, evaluation, control, V&V of controls, **overall residual risk**, **benefit–risk** where required, **risk management report(s)**, and **review** records.
3. The RMF may be **electronic** ([eQMS, SmartRisk, DMS path]) if access, version control, and backup meet **[SOP-DC-##]**, **[SOP-RC-##]**, and the electronic record policy, if any.

### Risk management planning (ISO 14971, 4.4; Clause 4)

- Before **[design input lock / D&D milestone – define]**, the Risk Management Lead shall **issue or update** the RMP.
- The RMP shall address **intended use**, user profile, **applicable consensus standards** (e.g. **[IEC 60601-1, IEC 62304, IEC 62366-1]**), and **hazards** from similar devices and **state of the art** (standards, literature, databases as appropriate).

### Hazard identification, risk analysis, and risk evaluation (ISO 14971, Clauses 5 and 6)

- **Hazard identification (e.g. 5.4):** identify **reasonably foreseeable** hazards, **hazardous situations,** sequences, and **harms** (including **[energy, chemical, biological, software, use error, misuse, lifecycle – as applicable]** as required by the RMP and **state of the art**).
- **Risk analysis (5):** **estimate and evaluate** risk per RMP (e.g. **FMEA,** **FTA,** **matrix**); keep **trace** from hazards and risks to design **outputs,** **V&V,** and released **documentation** as required by the D&D plan and **[SOP-### Design and development]**.
- **Risk evaluation (6):** compare to **risk acceptability** criteria; map unacceptable risks to **risk control** prior to **[design transfer / approval gate – define]** except where **documented** benefit–risk and regulatory requirements allow otherwise.

### Risk control (Clause 7)

- Apply controls in **priority order**: (1) **inherent safety**, (2) **protective measures** in device or process, (3) **information for safety** — the last **only** when residual risk cannot be reduced further by (1)–(2) and is **verified** for effectiveness.
- **Trace** each control to design/output evidence, **verification**, and **validation** when required.

### Residual risk and benefit–risk (ISO 14971, 8, 8.2)

- **Overall residual risk**: document in the **risk management report** (or equivalent).
- If overall residual risk is not acceptable, perform and retain **benefit–risk analysis** per ISO 14971 and applicable **jurisdictions**.


### Risk management report (ISO 14971)

- The Organization shall document a **risk management report** in the RMF per the **RMP** and **[template DOC-###,]** summarizing the process, key outputs, **overall** residual **risk,** and, when the RMP or applicable regulation requires, a **benefit–risk** conclusion for the **intended use** (including a structured **benefit–risk analysis** when required in **[region / NB / technical file SOP – define]**).

### Risk management review (Clause 9)

- **When**: prior to **[commercial / CE / first marketing – define]**; **[major design/process change, field action, PMS signal – define]**; when post-production data invalidate prior estimates.
- **Record**: participants/approvers, risk summary, decisions, and follow-up actions.

### Production and post-production information (Clause 10)

- **Complaints, vigilance, PMS, CAPA, service** per **[SOP-###, …]** shall supply **post-production** information to risk.
- On **triggers** above, the Risk Management Lead shall **[update RMF, change order, risk review – define]** within **[timeframe]**.

### Alignment with ISO 13485

- **7.3** Design/development: trace risk to **inputs, outputs, V&V, transfer** as applicable.
- **7.4 / 7.5** where supplier or process changes affect **safety**.
- **8.2.1 / 8.2.6** **Feedback** and **monitoring** as **inputs** to post-production risk updates.
- **Records** per **[SOP-RC-##]** and **[retention schedule – DOC-XXX]**.
