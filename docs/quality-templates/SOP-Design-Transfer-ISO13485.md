## Purpose

To define **[Company Legal Name]** (“the Organization”) requirements for **planning, authorizing, and documenting** **design transfer** so a **baselined** design is **released to manufacturing** (in-house, **CMO,** or **combination)** with **objective evidence** that **processes,** **personnel,** **equipment,** and **suppliers** can **consistently produce** and **release** **conforming** product, in accordance with **ISO 13485:2016, clause 7.3.8**.

---

## Scope

### In scope

- **Transfer** of **[medical devices, accessories, device software, SaMD — define]** from **design and development** to **routine manufacturing** and **service** (as applicable), as **gated** in the **design and development plan** **[SOP-DDP-###]**.
- **Transfer readiness review (TRR);** **manufacturing documentation;** **training;** **process, equipment,** and **supplier** **readiness;** and **handoff** **records** (DHF/DHR linked) per **[SOP-RC-##]**.
- **Re-transfer** or **delta** **transfer** after **significant** design or **site** changes per **[SOP-###]** and this SOP.

### Out of scope

- **Design inputs, outputs, review,** and **V&V** as primary processes — **[SOP-DI-##]**, **[SOP-DR-##]**, **[SOP-DV-##]**, **[SOP-DVal-##]**.
- **Process validation** **methodology** (IQ/OQ/PQ detail) — **[SOP-###]**; this SOP defines **readiness** and **handoff** to that work, not the validation protocol content itself.
- **General** **supplier** **onboarding** with no design release — **[SOP-###]**, except when **tied** to a **defined** **transfer** **milestone** for a **device.**

## Procedure

### 1. General (7.3.8)

1.1 **Design and development transfer** shall ensure that **design and development outputs** are **suitable** for **manufacturing,** **inspection,** and **servicing,** and that the organization can **verify** and **validate** (as **required)**** **those** **outputs,** in line with **ISO 13485:2016,** **7.3.8,** before **authorizing** **routine** **production** **(or** the **first** **build** **under** the **released** **DMR,** as **defined** in the **project)****.** **Document** the **conclusion** in a **handoff** **package** (see **Section** **8)****.**

1.2 **Transfer** is a **project-specific** **milestone** using **[template** DTR-**###]** or the **eQMS** **equivalent** and the **gates** in **[SOP-DDP-###]****.**

1.3 Where design is **transferred** to **external** **manufacturers,** **document** **agreements,** **change** **notification,** and **right** of **access** per **[SOP-###]**** **and** **7.3.8.**

### 2. Transfer readiness review (TRR)

2.1 Hold a **formal** **TRR** (or **equivalent** **design** **review** **topic)**** **before** **authorizing** the **first** **routine** **production** **build** **(or** **define** **equivalent** **milestone)**** **under** the **released** **DMR.**

2.2 **TRR** **agenda** **(minimum)****:**** **(a)**** **Design** **outputs** / **DMR** **index** — **complete,** **approved,** **released;** **(b)**** **DVer** / **DVal** **(as** **applicable)**** **and** **open** **deviations;** **(c)**** **RMF** **(including** **production** **and** **use-** **related** **controls)****;** **(d)**** **Labeling,** **UDI,** **IFU,** **translated** **docs** as **applicable;** **(e)**** **Readiness** **in** **Sections** **3–7;** **(f)**** **Regulatory** **(e.g.**** **device** **listing,** **GSPR)**** **as** **needed.**

2.3 **TRR** **outcome:**** **authorized,** **not** **authorized,** or **authorized** **with** **conditions** (each **condition,** **owner,** **due** **date)****.** **Do** **not** **start** **routine** **release** with **unclosed** **unacceptable** **gaps** without a **formal** **waiver** per **[SOP-###]**** **(risk-** **based)****.**

### 3. Manufacturing documentation

3.1 **Release** or **index** the **device** **master** **record** **(DMR)**** **/**** **DMI** per **[SOP-###]**** **to** the **transferred** **configuration,** including **as** **applicable:**** **drawings,** **BOM,** **specifications,** **routings,** **travellers,** **work** **instructions,** **packaging** and **labeling** **artwork,** **software** **version** and **build** **recipe,** **inspection** and **test** **criteria.**

3.2 For **software** or **firmware,** **define** **build,** **signing,** and **release** per **[SOP-###]**** **/**** **IEC** **62304** **and** **link** to **manufacturing** **SOPs.**

3.3 **After** **transfer,** **changes** to **DMR** or **manufacturing** **docs** follow **[SOP-###]****;** **re-transfer** or **delta** **DTR** when **required** by **risk** or **SOP-** **matrix.**

### 4. Training

4.1 **Before** **authorizing** **first** **routine** **build,** **identify** **roles** (operators, **inspectors,** **setup,** **maintenance,** **software** **release)**** **and** **require** **documented** **competence** per **[SOP-TR-##]**** **and** the **training** **matrix** in the **DTR** **package.**

4.2 **Record** **which** **SOP** / **WI** **revisions** **apply** to the **transferred** **device** or **line.**

4.3 For **CMO** **engagements,** **define** in the **quality** **agreement** **who** **trains,** **how** **records** are **exchanged,** and **how** **competence** is **demonstrated.**

### 5. Process readiness

5.1 **Manufacturing** and **special** **processes** (e.g. **sterilization,** **coating,** **bonding)**** **shall** have **defined** **routings,** a **control** **plan** (or **PFMEA** / **control** **matrix)****,** and **sampling** / **inspection** **points** before **TRR** **sign-**off, **unless** **formally** **exempt** with **rationale.**

5.2 **Process** **validation** **(IQ/OQ/PQ)**** **where** **required** by **regulation,** **risk,** or **SOP:**** **either** **complete** before **transfer** or **(if** **permitted)**** **a** **time-** **bound** **plan** with **interim** **controls** **approved** in the **TRR.**

5.3 **Line** **clearance,** **batch** **records,** and **DHR** **practices** **shall** **be** **ready** to support **lot** / **UDI-** **level** **trace** per **[SOP-###]**** **and** **7.5.**

### 6. Equipment readiness

6.1 **List** **gating** **equipment** (assembly, **test,** **inspection,** **sterilization,** **depyrogenation,** **etc.****)**** **in** the **DTR** / **TRR** **package.**

6.2 **Qualification** **(IQ** / **OQ** **or** **site-** **equivalent)**** **and** **calibration** / **PM** per **[SOP-###]**** **for** **equipment** on the **critical** **list;** **document** **gaps** or **shared-** **equipment** **rationale.**

6.3 **Automated** **test,** **software,** and **fixtures:**** **revision-** **controlled,** **verified** or **validated** as **per** **risk** and **[SOP-DV-##]**** **where** **applicable.**

### 7. Supplier readiness

7.1 For each **critical** or **strategic** **supplier** (including **CMO)**** **affecting** **conformity,** **document** **quality** **agreement,** **scope** of **work,** **right** of **access,** **change** **control,** and **first** **article** / **qualification** **status** per **[SOP-###]****.**

7.2 For **new** **materials** or **components** at **transfer,** **define** **incoming** **inspection,** **COA,** and **PPAP** or **equivalent** per **policy.**

7.3 **Storage** and **logistics** (e.g. **temperature)**** **shall** **be** **defined** and **aligned** with **IFU** and **labeling.**

### 8. Handoff records (DTR package)

8.1 **Maintain** a **Design** **Transfer** **Record** **(DTR)**** **or** **eQMS** **object** with **at** **minimum:**** **(a)**** **product,** **revision,** **transfer** **date;** **(b)**** **TRR** **approval** **/**** **minutes;** **(c)**** **pointers** to **DMR** **index** and **released** **docs;** **(d)**** **summary** of **process,** **equipment,** **supplier,** **and** **training** **readiness;** **(e)**** **residual** **actions** and **owners;** **(f)**** **signatories** (e.g.**** **R&D,** **Manufacturing,** **Quality)**** **as** **defined.**

8.2 **File** the **DTR** in the **DHF** (or **linked** **device** **file)**** **per** **[SOP-RC-##]****;** **retain** for **audits** and **postmarket** / **complaint** **investigation** **as** **needed.**

8.3 For **over-the-air,** **SaMD,** or **other** **field** or **service** **release** **paths,** **define** who **authorizes** **release** to **production** or **distribution** (may **cross-** **reference** **[SOP-###]**** **software** **release)**** **in** **8.1.**
