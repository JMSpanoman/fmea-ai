## Purpose

To define **[Company Legal Name]** (“the Organization”) requirements for **evaluating residual risk** after **risk controls** and for **performing and documenting benefit–risk analysis** when **required,** per **ISO 14971:2019** (Clause 8, including 8.2 as applicable) and the **risk management plan (RMP)**, with **evidence** in the **risk management file (RMF)** per **[SOP-RM-##]**.

This SOP **complements** **risk evaluation (Clause 6)**, **risk control (Clause 7)**, and the **RMP**. It defines **how** **individual** and **overall** **residual risks** are **assessed,** when **formal** **benefit–risk** is **required,** what **rationale** and **approvals** **apply,** and how **outputs** are **recorded** in the RMF (including the **risk management** **report** where used).

## Scope

### In scope

- **Individual** residual risk (per RMP structure, e.g. hazard, FMEA row, or software item) after implementation and V&V of controls per **SOP-R7-##** and the D&D plan
- **Overall** residual risk for the device (or RMP product family) and RMP **acceptability** conclusions
- **Benefit–risk** analysis when the RMP, jurisdiction, or **unacceptable** or **ALARP**-bounded residuals require it

### Out of scope

- **Non-**safety or marketing-only positioning not tied to RMP safety or clinical **benefit** (if **none,** state **none**)
- **Regulatory** **dossier** **format** and **notified** **body** **templates** (follow RA process and technical **documentation** **SOPs**)

**Justification** for out-of-scope items: **[RMP, quality plan, or RMF index]**

## Procedure

### 1. Preconditions (after risk control)

- Assess residual risk only after **implemented** risk controls and **RMP-defined** verification and/or validation (per **SOP-R7-##** and the D&D plan), unless the RMP explicitly allows a staged assessment.
- Re-estimate each line item (severity, probability, P1/P2, FMEA residual fields, etc.) per the RMP after controls; do not copy pre-control numbers as residual without RMP-defined mapping.

### 2. Individual residual risk

- For each individual residual (per RMP line or ID), compare the estimated residual to RMP **acceptability** criteria (see **SOP-RE-##**). Record the **conclusion** (acceptable, unacceptable, ALARP with follow-up, need for further **risk control,** or need for **benefit–risk** as the RMP requires).
- Unacceptable individual **residuals** must not be released without further **risk** **control,** a **documented** RMP **exception,** or a path to **Section** **4** (benefit–risk) when the RMP requires it.

### 3. Overall residual risk

- After individual residual conclusions are current, evaluate **overall residual risk** for the device (or RMP product family) per **ISO 14971:2019** Clause 8 and the RMP. Record the conclusion (e.g. acceptable, requires benefit–risk, not acceptable) in the RMF, often in or with the **risk management report** or an RMP-defined summary.

### 4. When formal benefit–risk analysis is required

- The RMP shall list **triggers** (e.g. unacceptable overall residual, serious hazard line, new indication, MDR/IVDR or notified body expectation).
- A **formal** benefit–risk (B–R) shall at minimum include: (a) intended use and patient or user population, (b) summary of relevant clinical, PMS, literature, or state of the art as the RMP defines, (c) identification and severity of relevant harms, (d) summary of benefit, (e) comparative weighing and conclusion, (f) residual uncertainties and mitigations.

### 5. Benefit–risk rationale and acceptability

- The B–R rationale shall be **objective** and **traceable** to **evidence**, not assertion alone, and aligned with the RMP and applicable jurisdictions on what counts as benefit and harm. Conflicts are escalated per the RMP and the regulatory process.

### 6. Approvals

- The RMP shall name who approves (a) individual and overall residual conclusions, and (b) B–R (e.g. risk lead, RAQA, clinical, top management for significant matters).
- **Signatures** (electronic or wet) per the **Organization**'s policy and **[SOP-### Electronic records / 21 CFR Part 11 as applicable]**, before RMP-defined release or filing milestones as applicable.

### 7. Documentation in the RMF

- The RMF shall contain or reference: (a) dated individual and overall residual assessments, (b) B–R when performed, (c) approvals and revision history, (d) links to V&V, labeling, and PMS as the RMP requires. List the **risk management report** and B–R in the RMF index per **[SOP-DC-##]** / **[SOP-RC-##]**.

### 8. Post-market and review

- Re-assess residuals and B–R when PMS, complaints, FSCA, or RMP triggers warrant, per **[SOP-RM-##]** and Clause 9 (risk management review).
