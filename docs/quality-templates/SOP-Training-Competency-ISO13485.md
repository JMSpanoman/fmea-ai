## Purpose

To define **[Company Legal Name]** (“the Organization”) requirements for **identifying training needs**, **delivering** training, **evaluating competency**, and **maintaining training records** so that personnel whose work affects **medical device** quality and **regulatory** conformity are **competent** and **aware** of their contribution to the **quality management system (QMS)**, in accordance with **ISO 13485:2016, clause 6.2**.

---

## Scope

### In scope

- **[Employees, temporary workers, contractors performing QMS-regulated work — define]** at **[sites / functions]**.
- Training and competency from **onboarding** through **ongoing** role requirements, **quality system** awareness, **refresher** cycles, and **effectiveness** verification where required by risk or procedure.
- **Training records** as **quality records** per **[SOP-RC-##]** and **[DOC-### Record Retention Schedule]**.

### Out of scope

- [General corporate training with no QMS or product impact — or state *none*.]
- [Board-only or investor-only sessions — or state *none*.]

## Procedure

### 1. Training needs identification

1.1 Training needs shall be identified from at least the following sources:

- **Job descriptions** and **role matrices** **[DOC-###]** (required skills and training codes per role).
- **New or changed positions** and **promotions** (HR notifies training administrator).
- **New or revised controlled documents** that change how work is performed, per **[SOP-DC-##]** (document owner flags affected roles).
- **CAPA**, **complaints**, **audit findings**, or **nonconformities** that indicate competence gaps.
- **New products, processes, equipment, or software** per **[SOP-Change-###]**.
- **Applicable regulatory** or **notified body** expectations (e.g. PRRC, vigilance basics, jurisdiction-specific rules).

1.2 Documented training needs shall be maintained in **[LMS / training matrix — DOC-###]** with course or module ID, target roles, frequency (initial and refresher), and competency evaluation method.

### 2. New hire training

2.1 Before **independent** performance of **QMS-affecting** tasks—or within **[X calendar days]** of start date if **supervised** work is permitted per **[WI-###]**—each new hire shall complete **[Company] new hire training**, including at minimum:

- Organization overview and code of conduct (if QMS-relevant).
- Introduction to the QMS: quality policy, structure, relevance of their work to conformity and patient safety.
- Applicable GxP expectations (e.g. data integrity, use of approved documents, escalation pathways).
- Site safety and access rules for regulated areas, if applicable.

2.2 **Role-specific** training (§3) and **quality system** training (§4) shall be assigned per the approved matrix before or in parallel with supervised work as defined in **[WI-###]**.

2.3 Supervisors shall not assign **unsupervised critical tasks** until competency requirements in §6 are met for those tasks.

### 3. Role-specific training

3.1 Role-specific training covers tasks unique to the function (e.g. incoming inspection, sterilization operation, software build and release, complaint intake).

3.2 Content shall be derived from **released** SOPs and WIs; training material revision shall align with current controlled documents per **[SOP-DC-##]**.

3.3 Delivery may include classroom, e-learning, **on-the-job (OJT)** with a **qualified trainer**, or external providers. External courses shall be pre-approved by **[HR / process owner]** when cost or content exceeds **[threshold]**. Certificates of completion shall be filed per §8.

### 4. Quality system training

4.1 All personnel affecting product quality shall receive **QMS awareness** training covering at minimum:

- Quality policy and objectives.
- Relevance of their work to the QMS and device conformity.
- Implications of not following documented procedures.
- Where and how to report problems (nonconformity, complaint, adverse event pathways as applicable).

4.2 **Initial** QMS awareness: within **[X days]** of start or before independent regulated work, per §2. **Refresher**: per §5.

4.3 QMS training content shall be updated when significant QMS changes occur (e.g. major procedure releases, merger, new site) and at least **[annual / biennial — define]**.

### 5. Refresher training

5.1 Refresher intervals shall be defined per course in **[training matrix DOC-###]**. Examples (replace with approved values):

| Topic class | Example refresher |
|-------------|---------------------|
| QMS awareness | [e.g. every 2 years] |
| Critical process (e.g. cleanroom, sterilization load release) | [e.g. annual] |
| Regulatory topic (e.g. vigilance basics) | [on regulatory change or annual] |

5.2 LMS or HR shall issue reminders **[n days]** before due date. If training expires without approved extension from **[Quality / HR]**, supervisors shall restrict or reassign work until training is current.

5.3 Refresher may be **delta** (changes-only) training when documented in **[WI-###]** and risk is assessed as low.

### 6. Competency evaluation

6.1 **Competency** means demonstrated ability to perform assigned tasks correctly, not only completion of a course.

6.2 Evaluation methods shall be selected per risk and role in **[matrix]**, and may include one or more of:

- Written or online assessment (passing score **[define]**).
- Witnessed demonstration of performance by a qualified trainer.
- Completed OJT checklist with defined sign-offs.
- External certification where required for the role.

6.3 Supervisor **authorization for independent work** shall be recorded in **[LMS / FORM-###]** after §6.2 criteria are met.

6.4 Re-evaluation is required after prolonged absence **[define months]**, significant process change affecting the task, or CAPA requiring retraining.

### 7. Effectiveness checks

7.1 Training **effectiveness** shall be evaluated where training mitigates significant risk to product or patient safety. **Critical processes** requiring effectiveness checks shall be listed in **[DOC-###]**.

7.2 Effectiveness methods may include:

- Quiz or practical exam with documented pass criteria (immediate check).
- Trend review of errors, scrap, complaints, or audit findings attributable to the role within **[e.g. 90 days]** post-training.
- Second-person verification for a defined period after new task authorization.

7.3 If effectiveness is not achieved, supervisor and Quality shall initiate **[CAPA / supplemental training per SOP-###]** and restrict task authorization until appropriately closed.

### 8. Training documentation and records

8.1 For each completed training event, the Organization shall maintain at minimum:

- Trainee identity (unique employee or contractor ID).
- Course or module title and revision (or SOP revision covered).
- Delivery method and duration if required by regulation or procedure.
- Date completed.
- Instructor or system identifier (for e-learning).
- Assessment result, if applicable.
- Competency authorization sign-off, if applicable.

8.2 Records shall reside in **[LMS / controlled repository]** with access controls per **[IT policy]**. Audit exports shall be legible and complete.

8.3 Retention and disposition follow **[SOP-RC-##]** and **[DOC-### Record Retention Schedule]**.

8.4 Electronic signatures in the LMS shall meet **[SOP-### / 21 CFR Part 11 if US]**.
